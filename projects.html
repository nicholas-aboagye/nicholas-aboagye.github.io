Custom web app · JavaScript

US Heart Disease Rate — interactive state explorer

A hand-coded mapping application rather than a configured template. A state list filters live to whatever is in the current view extent; hovering a state highlights it and opens its popup; states can be multi-selected and highlighted together; each has a zoom-to control with geometry and extent fallbacks; and clicking the map reads out longitude and latitude to six decimal places. It runs on the same web map as the story below — the same data, built twice, once by configuration and once in code.

Research projects — completed

MA thesis · 2022–24

25 years of land cover change, Northern Rio Grande Corridor

Multi-temporal Random Forest classification of Landsat imagery, 1995–2020. Leaf-on, leaf-off and index-enhanced models were compared, then combined in an elevation-stratified post-classification composite — split at 2,000 m — that mosaics separate lowland and highland models to raise overall accuracy.
